Programme overview

A live, instructor-led SQL programme equipping MBAs to query data directly and challenge the numbers they’re given. Each cohort runs as two 3-hour sessions against a live PostgreSQL database. Especially valuable for MBAs heading into product or tech roles.

What's included · per cohort
  • Two 3-hour live, instructor-led sessions (Foundations + Intermediate)
  • Hosted training database and browser-based pgAdmin access, provisioned per cohort — no installs required
  • Worked examples, guided exercises and takeaway slide decks from both sessions
  • Session recordings shared with all participants afterwards
  • Dedicated instructor and a single point of contact for the engagement
Curriculum at a glance
Part 1Foundations SELECT / FROM / WHERE, comparison & pattern-matching operators (BETWEEN, ILIKE), ORDER BY, COUNT & DISTINCT, aggregates with GROUP BY, aliasing, and CASE WHEN logic.
Part 2AI & Applications JOINs (INNER, LEFT, RIGHT & FULL), subqueries & common table expressions, window functions (RANK, LAG, LEAD), and using GenAI to write, explain and validate SQL.

  • Use AI to go faster while reading and validating what it writes
